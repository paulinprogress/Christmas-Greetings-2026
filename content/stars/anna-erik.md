## Star facts

- **Catalogue no.:** XMAS-EE3A55
- **Shines like:** Tau Ceti, in the constellation Cetus
- **Distance from Earth:** 11.9 light-years (about 1.1 × 10¹⁴ km)
- **The light you see left in:** the year 2014
- **Time to fly there by jet:** about 14.3 million years
- **Spectral type:** G8V
- **Brightness:** magnitude 3.5 (lower means brighter)
- **Fun fact:** A Sun-like star that has long been a favourite target for alien-hunters.
